# The Truth Gate

One of the biggest flaws in modern AI is memory corruption. When an AI learns automatically from every interaction, it is vulnerable to manipulation, hallucinations, and drifting away from its core identity. 

Siduri approaches reality differently.

## Vague Concept: The Operator & The Anchor
Siduri does not blindly accept every interaction as absolute truth. Instead, she utilizes a dual-layered reality system.

While she maintains short-term conversational awareness naturally, any fundamental changes to her understanding of the world, her relationships, or her own persona must pass through a strict **Truth Gate**. 

This means that behind the scenes, there is a mechanism for a human operator to audit, approve, or reject her shifting worldview before it permanently alters her core database. She remains anchored to the reality you curate for her, ensuring she never loses her designated identity—even when exposed to thousands of conflicting inputs in a live digital environment.
